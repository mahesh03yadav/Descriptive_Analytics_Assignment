# 🍔 McDonald's Sales – Descriptive Analytics

### 📊 Exploring Sales Trends, Menu Performance & Customer Satisfaction

A data analytics project focused on analyzing McDonald's sales transactions to uncover insights into revenue trends, menu item popularity, order patterns, payment methods, store performance, and customer satisfaction.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs descriptive analytics on a dataset containing **10,000 transaction records** covering January 2024 to December 2025.

The objective is to transform transaction-level data into meaningful business insights through data analysis, statistical techniques, and visualizations.

**Note:** The dataset is synthetic and intended for analytical and educational purposes. The results do not represent McDonald's actual sales or operations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Understand average order value patterns.
- Identify popular menu items.
- Examine order types and payment methods.
- Compare store and city performance.
- Analyze discounts and customer satisfaction.
- Explore relationships between sales variables.
- Identify unusual transactions.
- Develop data-driven business recommendations.

## 📈 Key Findings

### 💰 Sales & Revenue
- Total net revenue: **$73,439.28**
- Average order value: **$7.34**
- September recorded the highest average order value at **$7.92**.
- February recorded the lowest average order value at **$6.91**.

### 🍔 Menu Performance
- Egg McMuffin was the most frequently ordered menu item, with **429 orders**.
- Big Mac ranked second, with **414 orders**.
- Food items accounted for **74% of all orders**.

### 🚗 Order Channels
- Delivery was the most common order type, representing **20.57% of orders**.
- Drive-Thru followed closely at **20.52%**.
- Order volume was distributed relatively evenly across the five recorded channels.

### 💳 Payment Analysis
- Gift Card was the most frequently used payment method, accounting for **20.19% of orders**.
- Average satisfaction ratings varied only slightly across payment methods.

### ⭐ Customer Satisfaction
- Average satisfaction rating: **4.09/5**
- Approximately **40% of orders received a rating of 5**.
- Only **6.9% received a rating of 1 or 2**.

### 🏪 Store & City Performance
- Nashville recorded the highest city-level net revenue at **$4,171.40**.
- Store McD-0058 recorded the highest order volume, with **88 orders**.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis suggests several opportunities for restaurant operations:

- Prioritize staffing and inventory during peak order hours.
- Evaluate discounts based on order volume and traffic rather than assuming they improve satisfaction.
- Maintain operational support across all order channels.
- Monitor unusual transactions separately from regular averages.
- Use menu popularity to guide future product analysis.
- Investigate customer feedback to identify opportunities for service improvement.

## ⚠️ Limitations
